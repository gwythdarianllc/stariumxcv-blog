We spread our efforts across maps, interactions, and UI this month. Here’s what’s new.

#### The Galactic Map

One of the most exciting changes to come out of the client rebuild is that the galactic map is now unbounded. There's no longer a fixed view frame limiting what you can see. Players will be able to pan freely across the entire known galaxy, with stars they've visited visible alongside stars that fall within their ships' sensor range.

![](/assets/the-galactic-map.png)

#### Army & Fleet Interactions

We've implemented base-level functionality for armies and fleets. Movement, claiming & settling areas, and visibility is all working. It doesn't look its best yet, but everything is behaving the way it should.
